\section{Research Summary}
\cvitem{}{My research is in algorithms for massive data: coresets and data
summarization, sublinear and streaming algorithms, and metric embeddings, with a
focus on clustering. At the Weizmann Institute, together with Robert Krauthgamer,
I introduced \emph{stable coresets}, a guarantee between weak and strong coresets,
and showed that plain uniform sampling achieves it with size independent of the
number of points. This yields new coresets for $\ell_1$ and related metrics, with
applications to fair clustering and $k$-median approximation (ICLR 2026), and
optimal stable coresets for the geometric median (APPROX 2026). Related work
studies coresets for 1-center in $\ell_1$ metrics (ITCS 2025, TALG 2026) and coresets for
clustering from noisy comparisons with few distance queries (NeurIPS 2026). At
Penn State I worked on streaming algorithms for fitting tree metrics and
ultrametrics (ICALP 2025) and on weighted rank aggregation (ICALP 2026). My PhD
was in combinatorial pattern matching and computational biology.}
